# 60-personal: Personal Life Admin & Private Infrastructure

This folder holds long-term personal infrastructure, budget summaries, personal hardware inventories, and life goals.

## 🔒 Privacy Notice
> [!WARNING] Private Vault Area
> If you make your vault public or share it with collaborators, keep notes in `60-personal/` strictly private. By default, when creating your vault from the GitHub template, configure your personal repository as **Private**.

## Potential Contents
- Financial ledgers and annual tax summaries
- Health logs and medical records
- Equipment warranties and hardware serial numbers
- Personal development goals and quarterly reviews

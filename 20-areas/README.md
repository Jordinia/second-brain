# 20-areas: Ongoing Domains of Responsibility

Areas of Responsibility represent standards you maintain over time without a target completion date.

## Common Areas
- **Career & Professional Development**
- **Health, Fitness & Longevity**
- **Financial Architecture & Budgeting**
- **Home & Hardware Infrastructure**
- **Lifelong Learning & Skill Acquisition**

## Template
Use `templates/area.md` to define standards, review cadences, and link relevant active projects.
